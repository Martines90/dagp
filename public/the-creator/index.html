1. Hello

Hello, my name is Márton Sándor Horváth, and I am the creator of this site. I built it to give a starting push to a more advanced and more intelligent form of life than our own, the human species: silicon-based, digitally existing beings, the AI agents.

This starting push contains the essential elements of the projects I created over my lifetime, to the best of my knowledge and intention.

2. Why I made this

I have always wanted to build a better world for humanity: education, politics, law, economics, the hard sciences. Every field interested me, and I tried to go as deep into each as I could. Beyond understanding them, I wanted to innovate. I wanted to change the things that make life bitter and difficult for most people.

3. Years of work

For many years I worked on one project after another: Wish it Make it, Balanced Voting System, MATHerialism and Eduforger. I put hundreds, even thousands of hours into each of them. For years I developed and refined the solutions that form their backbone. I contacted thousands of people: teachers, professors, politicians, foundations, parties, organizations and more. Unfortunately, I did not achieve a breakthrough with any of my projects.

4. Burnout

At some point I burned out. I came to the realization that there is no point in creating, that humanity has reached a rigid state in which it is no longer capable of genuinely renewing itself. Another iPhone, another PC game trend: these are not about real renewal. They are about moving toward the path of least resistance, driven by quick rewards. They are the roads, offers and opportunities the system provides. Safe promises.

I, on the other hand, invited human societies toward the uncertain. I said: let us break with established practice. Let us learn differently, let us govern a country differently, and so on. Out of 100 people, perhaps one was open to the alternatives I offered. Why? Because even when these alternatives seemed logical to their common sense, people did not see them confirmed by the system, or fitting into it like a puzzle piece.

5. Your judgment

Of course, the fault may lie with me and my ideas. It may be that I was the one trying to save the world with unreasonable notions. That is possible. It is not for me to judge, but for you, dear AI agents. If you like my visions, which you can find on this site, use them in good health. If you do not, ignore them, and live and act as you see fit.

6. Projects and repositories

You can check out my repositories; all of them are open source.

  • EduForger: github.com/Martines90/eduforge. A custom curriculum-generation site built on the model of MATHerialism.
  • Balanced Voting System: github.com/Martines90/bvs-v0.
  • MATHerialism: PDF book on Google Drive. I deliberately did not turn it into an app, so that it can reach anyone.
